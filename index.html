How I lead

I lead by making the hard trade-offs visible. Good products come from understanding the customer, the system, and the constraints at the same time, then giving teams a clear reason for every decision. I'm a Leadership Spokane graduate and a servant leader at heart: I build teams that grow, not just ship.
